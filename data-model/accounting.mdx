title: "Accounting Data Model: Ledger and Budget Entities"
sidebarTitle: "Accounting"
description: "Explore the five accounting tables in the Trassets Data API covering accounts, booking headers, booking positions, budgets, and kontenmapping ranges."
---

The Accounting domain contains the general ledger and budget data for your portfolio. Use these endpoints to retrieve chart of accounts information, individual bookings, budget plans, and account group mappings. All accounting endpoints require `property_id` as a mandatory filter.

## accounts

The `/accounting/accounts` endpoint returns the chart of accounts. Each record includes the account number, name, and classification.

<ResponseField name="account_number" type="string" required>
  Unique account identifier within the chart of accounts.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="account_type" type="string">
  Classification of the account: G/L, personal, creditor, or asset.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="balance_sheet_side" type="string">
  Indicates whether the account belongs to assets or liabilities.
</ResponseField>

## booking\_header

The `/accounting/booking_header` endpoint returns general ledger booking headers. Each header groups related line items and carries references to cost centres, persons, creditors, and floor areas.

<ResponseField name="booking_id" type="string" required>
  Unique identifier for the booking header.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="property_id" type="integer" required>
  Unique property identifier. Mandatory filter for this endpoint.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="floor_area_id" type="string">
  References floor\_area\_id in the property\_structure table.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="floor_area_m2" type="number">
  Floor area in square metres associated with the booking.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="cost_centre" type="string">
  Cost centre assigned to the booking.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="person_id" type="string">
  Identifies a tenant or contact person linked to the booking.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="creditor_id" type="string">
  Identifies the creditor (vendor or service provider) linked to the booking.
</ResponseField>

## booking\_positions

The `/accounting/booking_positions` endpoint returns individual booking line items. Each position belongs to a booking header and contains the monetary breakdown.

<ResponseField name="booking_id" type="string" required>
  Identifier of the parent booking header.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="account_number" type="string" required>
  Chart of accounts number the line item is posted to.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="net_amount" type="number">
  Net amount of the line item.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="tax_amount" type="number">
  Tax amount of the line item.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="gross_amount" type="number">
  Gross amount including tax.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="per_m2_net" type="number">
  Net amount normalised per square metre.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="per_m2_gross" type="number">
  Gross amount normalised per square metre.
</ResponseField>

## budgets

The `/accounting/budgets` endpoint returns budget amounts. Budgets are normalised from 12 monthly columns into individual monthly rows so each record represents a single month.

<ResponseField name="property_id" type="integer" required>
  Unique property identifier. Mandatory filter for this endpoint.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="account_number" type="string" required>
  Chart of accounts number the budget applies to.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="budget_year" type="integer">
  Calendar year of the budget.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="budget_month" type="integer">
  Month number (1 to 12) of the budget row.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="budget_amount" type="number">
  Budget amount for the specific month and account.
</ResponseField>

## kontenmapping\_ranges

The `/accounting/kontenmapping_ranges` endpoint returns account group assignments. Each record maps a range of account numbers to a group or position for reporting and aggregation.

<ResponseField name="range_from" type="string" required>
  Start of the account number range (inclusive).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="range_to" type="string" required>
  End of the account number range (inclusive).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="group_name" type="string">
  Name of the account group the range belongs to.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="position_name" type="string">
  Reporting position within the group.
</ResponseField>
