The Market Data domain provides benchmark and comparable market data for real estate assets. Use these endpoints to compare your portfolio against market indicators, analyse rent levels, and assess asset performance relative to the broader market.

Market Data records are typically referenced by property identifiers so you can correlate market benchmarks with your own real-estate, contract, and accounting data.

<Note>
  The exact endpoint names and response schemas for Market Data are defined in the OpenAPI specification. See the **API Reference** tab for the full list of Market Data endpoints, request parameters, and response fields.
</Note>
