All category endpoints of the Data API live under `/v1/`, e.g.:

```text
https://api.trassets.ai/v1/accounting/budgets
```

Every `/v1/` route follows the [response structure](/v1/response-shape-conventions) rules. For the current list of available tables, check the **API Reference** tab.

<Warning>
  Breaking changes never land on a published path. They come under a new prefix such as `/v2/`.
</Warning>

## Endpoints without a version prefix

`GET /catalog` and `GET /changes` exist once for the whole API and carry no prefix.

Raw tables are available under both `/v1/raw/{table_name}` and `/raw/{table_name}` — same data. The row count is in `total` under `/v1/` and in `total_rows` without the prefix.

## Announced tables

`/v1/operations/offers` and `/v1/operations/orders` are announced and will follow in a later release.
